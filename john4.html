“Sir” and “Lord”

“Sir” here is kyrie, the same Greek word that is “Lord” elsewhere: a polite way to address a man, and also the word for the Lord. This translation says “Sir” where someone speaks to Jesus before knowing who he is, and “Lord” where a disciple or believer speaks; “Lord” everywhere would decide the speakers’ faith for them.
