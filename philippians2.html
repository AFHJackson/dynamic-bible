So, if there is any encouragement in Christ, if there is any comfort from agapēs , if there is any sharing in pneumatos , if there is any affection and compassion, fill my joy by thinking the same thing, having the same agapēn , united in soul, thinking as one. Do nothing out of rivalry or out of empty glory, but in humility count one another as surpassing yourselves, each of you looking not to your own interests, but each of you also to the interests of heterōn . Think this among yourselves, which was also in Christ Jesus: who, existing in God’s form, did not count being equal to God something to seize, but emptied himself, taking the form of a doulou , becoming like humans, and being found in shape as a human, he humbled himself, becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross. For this reason God also raised him to the highest place, and freely gave him the name above every name, so that at the name of Jesus every knee would bend— of those in heaven and on earth and under the earth— and every tongue would openly acknowledge that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

So, my agapētoi , just as you have always obeyed, not only when I am present but now much more when I am absent, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For God is the one working in you, both the willing and the working, for his good will.

Do everything without grumbling and arguing, so that you may become blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish among a crooked and twisted generation, among whom you shine like lights in kosmō holding firmly to the logon of zōēs , so that I may have reason to boast on the day of Christ that I did not run in vain or work hard in vain. But even if I am being poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and service of your pisteōs , I rejoice and rejoice with all of you. And you too, do the same: rejoice and rejoice with me.

But I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, so that I too may be encouraged, gnous how you are doing. For I have no one of the same mind who will sincerely care about how you are doing. For they all seek their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ. But you ginōskete his proven character, because like a child with a father, he served with me for the gospel. So I hope to send him as soon as I see how things stand with me, without delay. But I am confident in the Lord that I myself will also come soon.

But I counted it necessary to send Epaphroditus to you, my brother and coworker and fellow soldier, and your messenger and one who serves my need, because he was longing for all of you and was distressed because you heard that he had been sick. Indeed, he was sick, close to death. But God had mercy on him, and not only him but also me, so that I would not have sorrow upon sorrow. So I sent him all the more eagerly, so that when you see him again, you may rejoice, and I may be less sorrowful. So welcome him in the Lord with all joy, and hold people like him in honor, because for the work of Christ he came close to death, risking his psychē to make up what was lacking in your service to me.
