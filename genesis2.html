And the heavens and the earth and all their array were completed. And elohim finished on the seventh day his work that he had done. And he stopped on the seventh day from all his work that he had done. And elohim yvarekh the seventh day and made it holy, because on it he stopped from all his work that elohim had created to do. These are the generations of the skies and the earth when they were created, on the day Yahweh elohim made earth and skies. And no shrub of the field was yet in the earth, and no plant of the field had yet sprouted, because Yahweh elohim had not sent rain upon the earth, and there was no human to work the ground. And a mist would rise from the earth and water the whole surface of the ground. Then Yahweh elohim formed the human from dust from the ground and blew into his nostrils nishmat life, and the human became a living being. And Yahweh elohim planted a garden in Eden to the east, and placed there the human whom he had formed. And Yahweh elohim made grow from the ground every tree pleasing to the eye and good for food, and the tree of life in the middle of the garden, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. And a river flowed out from Eden to water the garden, and from there it divided and became four branches. The name of the first is Pishon . It is the one that winds around all the land of Havilah , where there is gold. And the gold of that land is good. There are bdellium and stone of onyx. And the name of the second river is Gihon . It is the one that winds around all the land of Cush . And the name of the third river is Tigris . It is the one that flows east of Asshur . And the fourth river is Euphrates . Then Yahweh elohim took the human and settled him in the garden of Eden to cultivate it and to guard it. Then Yahweh elohim commanded the man, saying, “You may freely eat from every tree in the garden. But from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, you must not eat from it, because on the day you eat from it, you will certainly die.” Then Yahweh elohim said, “It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make for him a helper who corresponds to him.” Then Yahweh elohim formed from the ground every animal of the field and every bird of the sky, and brought them to the man to see what he would call each one. And whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name. And the man gave names to all the livestock, and to the birds of the sky, and to every animal of the field. But for Adam he did not find a helper who corresponded to him. Then Yahweh elohim caused a deep sleep to fall upon the man, and he slept. Then he took one of his ribs and closed up the flesh in its place. Then Yahweh elohim built the rib that he had taken from the man into a woman and brought her to the man. And the human said, “This time, this one is bone from my bones and flesh from my flesh. This one will be called woman, because this one was taken from man.” That is why a man leaves his father and his mother and clings to his wife, and they become one flesh. And the man and his wife were both naked, and were not ashamed.
