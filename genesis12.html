Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot , his brother's son, and all their possessions that they had acquired, and the people that they had acquired in Haran . They set out to go to the land of Canaan , and they arrived in the land of Canaan . Abram passed through the land as far as the place of Shechem , as far as the oak of Moreh . The Canaanites were then in the land. Yahweh appeared to Abram and said, “I will give this land to your descendants.” He built an altar there to Yahweh , who had appeared to him. He moved from there to the hill country east of Bethel and pitched his tent, with Bethel to the west and the Ai to the east. He built an altar there to Yahweh and called on the name of Yahweh . Abram set out, continuing to travel toward the south. There was a famine in the land. Abram went down to Egypt to stay as a foreigner there, because the famine was severe in the land. As he drew near to go into Egypt , he said to Sarai , his wife, “Look, I know that you are a woman beautiful in appearance. When the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘This is his wife.’ Then they will kill me, but you they will keep alive. Please say you are my sister, so that things will go well for me because of you, and my life will be preserved because of you.” When Abram came into Egypt , the Egyptians saw the woman, that she was very beautiful. The officials of Pharaoh saw her and yhalu her to Pharaoh , and the woman was taken to the house of Pharaoh . And to Abram he did good because of her. And he came to have sheep and goats, cattle, male donkeys, male slaves, female slaves, female donkeys, and camels. And Yahweh afflicted Pharaoh and his household with severe afflictions over the devar Sarai , the wife of Abram . Then Pharaoh called to Abram and said, “What is this you have done to me? Why didn't you tell me that she is your wife?” “Why did you say, 'She is my sister'? So I took her for myself as a wife. And now, here is your wife. Take her and go!” Then Pharaoh gave orders to men concerning him, and they sent him and his wife and everything that belonged to him away.
