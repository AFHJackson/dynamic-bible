Now pistis is the assurance of what is hoped for, the proof of things not blepomenōn . For through this the people of earlier times received approval. Through pistei we understand that the aiōnas were put in order by God's rhēmati , so that what is blepomenon has come into being from things that do not appear.

Through pistei Abel offered God a greater sacrifice than Cain, through which he was attested to be righteous, God testifying about his gifts. And through it, though he died, he still speaks. Through pistei Enoch was taken away so that he would not idein death, and he could not be found because God had taken him away. For before his removal, he had been attested to have pleased God. By pistei Noah, warned about things not yet blepomenōn , acted with reverent care and built an ark to save his household. Through this he condemned the kosmon and became an heir of the righteousness that accords with pistin .

By pistei Abraham, when called, obeyed and went out to a place that he was going to receive as an inheritance, and he went out without knowing where he was going. By pistei he lived as a foreign resident in the land of the promise, as in a foreign land, living in tents with Isaac and Jacob, fellow heirs of the same promise. For he was waiting for the city that has foundations, whose designer and builder is God. By pistei , even with Sarah herself, he received dynamin for depositing seed, even beyond the kairon of age, since he considered the one who promised piston . That is why from just one man, and one already as good as dead, there were also born as many as the stars in the sky and as the countless sand along the seashore.

All these people died in keeping with pistin , without receiving the promises, but having idontes them from far away and welcomed them, and having acknowledged that they are strangers and temporary residents on the earth. For those who say such things make it clear that they are seeking a homeland. And if they keep remembering that homeland from which they departed, they would have had kairon to return. But now they long for a better place, that is, a heavenly one. That is why God is not ashamed of them, to be called their God, because he prepared a city for them.

By pistei Abraham offered Isaac when he was being tested, and the one who had received the promises was offering his only son, the one to whom it was said, “Through Isaac offspring will be named for you.” He reasoned that God was able to raise someone even from the dead; from there he also received him back, figuratively speaking.

By pistei Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau also concerning things to come. By pistei Jacob, as he was dying, blessed each of Joseph's sons and bowed in worship over the top of his staff. By pistei Joseph, as he was dying, spoke about the departure of the people of Israel and gave instructions about his bones.

By pistei Moses was hidden for three months after his birth by his parents, because they eid on the child was beautiful, and they were not afraid of the king's decree. By pistei Moses, when he grew up, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter, choosing to suffer mistreatment together with God's people rather than have the temporary enjoyment of hamartias . He considered the disgrace of Christ greater wealth than Egypt's treasures, because he was looking ahead to the reward.

By pistei he left Egypt, without fearing the king's anger, because he held firm as though horōn the invisible one. By pistei he has observed the Passover and the sprinkling of blood, so that the one destroying the firstborn would not touch them. By pistei they crossed the Red Sea as though across dry land; the Egyptians, trying the same crossing, were swallowed up.

By pistei the walls of Jericho fell after being encircled for seven days. By pistei Rahab the prostitute did not perish together with those who disobeyed, having welcomed the spies in peace.

And what more should I say? For chronos will run out for me as I tell about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David and Samuel and the prophets, who, through pisteōs , defeated kingdoms, practiced righteousness, obtained promises, shut the mouths of lions, extinguished the dynamin of fire, escaped the edges of the sword, were made strong out of weakness, became mighty in battle, put to flight the armies of foreigners.

Women received their dead back through resurrection. But alloi were tortured, refusing release, so that they might obtain a better resurrection. But heteroi experienced mockery and whippings, and even chains and imprisonment. They were stoned, sawn apart, and died by slaughter with a sword. They went around in sheepskins, in goatskins, lacking necessities, oppressed, mistreated. The kosmos was not worthy of them. They wandered through desolate places and mountains and caves and holes in the ground.

And all of them, though commended through pisteōs did not receive what was promised, because God had planned something better for us, so that they would not be made complete without us.
