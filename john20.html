Our Greek text

Our Greek text (SBLGNT) has the present tense here and in 19:35: “so that you may go on believing”. Many manuscripts have a form that means “come to believe”, and most English Bibles say simply “may believe”. Scholars debate whether the tense shows that John wrote for believers or for those who did not yet believe; most agree the tense alone cannot settle it.
