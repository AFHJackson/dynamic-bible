The last Passover (πάσχα)

The final Passover is counted down: "six days before the Passover" (12:1), "before the feast of the Passover" (13:1), the accusers wish to "eat the Passover" (18:28), the custom of release "at the Passover" (18:39), "the day of preparation of the Passover" (19:14).
