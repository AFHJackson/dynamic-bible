Love of strangers (philoxenia): Romans 12 and Hebrews 13

φιλοξενία, love of strangers, occurs in the New Testament only in these two verses, both in a list of commands about life together: "pursue hospitality" (Romans 12:13); "do not forget to welcome strangers" (Hebrews 13:2). The same Greek word is saved as "hospitality" in Romans 12 and as "welcome strangers" in Hebrews 13 (to keep the play on "welcomed angels").
