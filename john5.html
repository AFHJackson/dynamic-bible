Verse 5:4

Some later manuscripts add words at the end of 5:3 and a verse 5:4: the sick were waiting for the water to move, because an angel went down into the pool at certain times and stirred the water, and whoever stepped in first after the stirring was healed. The oldest manuscripts do not have these words, and our Greek text (SBLGNT, like NA28) leaves them out, so there is no verse 5:4 here. The sick man’s answer in 5:7 assumes the belief about the stirred water.
