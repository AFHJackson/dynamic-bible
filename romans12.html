So I urge you, brothers and sisters, by God's compassion, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God— your rational service of worship. And do not shape yourselves to this aiōni , but be transformed through the renewal of your mind, so that you can test and discern what God's will is— what is agathon and pleasing and complete.

For I say, through the grace given to me, to everyone among you: do not think more highly of yourself than you should think, but think with sound judgment, each according to the measure of pisteōs God has assigned. For just as we have many parts in one body, and all the parts do not have the same function, so we, the many, are one body in Christ, and individually parts of one another.

We have gifts that differ according to the grace given to us: if prophecy, in proportion to pisteōs if service, in serving; if someone teaches, in teaching; if someone encourages, in encouraging; whoever shares, with generosity; whoever leads, with diligence; whoever shows mercy, with cheerfulness.

Let agapē be sincere. Detest what is evil; hold tightly to agathō In sibling affection, be philostorgoi toward one another; take the lead in honoring one another. Do not be lazy in your diligence; be burning in pneymati ; serve the Lord. Rejoice in hope; endure in hardship; persist in proseychē . Share in meeting the needs of the holy people; actively pursue hospitality.

Bless those who persecute; bless and do not curse. Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep. Think the same way toward one another. Do not set your minds on lofty things, but go along with humble people. Do not become wise in your own estimation.

Repay no one evil for evil. Give thought to kala in everyone's sight. If possible, as far as it depends on you, live peacefully with everyone. Do not avenge yourselves, agapētoi , but make room for wrath, for it is written: “Vengeance belongs to me; I will repay,” says the Lord. But if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink. For by doing this, you will pile burning coals on his head. Do not be defeated by evil, but defeat evil with agathō .
