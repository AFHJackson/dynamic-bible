The man had sex with Eve , his wife, and she became pregnant and gave birth to Cain . She said, “I have acquired a man with Yahweh .” Then she went on to give birth to his brother, Abel . Abel became a herder of sheep and goats, and Cain was a worker of the ground. After some time, Cain brought some produce from the ground as an offering to Yahweh . And Abel also brought some of the firstborn of his flock and some of their fat. Yahweh looked with favor on Abel and on his offering. But on Cain and on his offering he did not look with favor. Then anger burned very strongly for Cain , and his face fell. Then Yahweh said to Cain , “Why are you angry? And why has your face fallen? If you do well, won't there be a lifting up? But if you do not do well, chatat is crouching at the doorway. Its desire is toward you, and you will rule over it.” Then Cain spoke to Abel , his brother. And it happened that while they were in the field, Cain rose up against Abel , his brother, and killed him. Then Yahweh said to Cain , “Where is Abel , your brother?” He said, “I don't know. Am I my brother's guard?” He said, “What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground!” And now you are cursed away from the ground, which opened its mouth to receive your brother's blood from your hand. When you work the ground, it will no longer give its strength to you. You will be wandering and roaming on the earth. Cain said to Yahweh , “My 'avon is too great to bear.” “Look, you have driven me today away from the face of the ground, and I will be hidden from your face. I will be wandering and roaming on the earth, and anyone who finds me will kill me.” Yahweh said to him, “Therefore, anyone who kills Cain will be avenged seven times over.” Yahweh then set a sign for Cain so that anyone who found him would not strike him. Cain went out from the presence of Yahweh and settled in the land of Nod east of Eden . Cain had sex with his wife, and she became pregnant and gave birth to Enoch . He was building a city, and he called the name of the city by the name of his son, Enoch . Irad was born to Enoch . And Irad fathered Mehujael . And Mehujael fathered Methushael . And Methushael fathered Lamech . Lamech took for himself two wives. The name of one was Adah , and the name of the second was Zillah . Adah gave birth to Jabal . He was the father of those who live in tents and raise livestock. And his brother's name was Jubal . He was the father of all who play the lyre and pipe. And Zillah also gave birth to Tubal Cain , a sharpener for every worker in bronze and iron. And the sister of Tubal Cain was Naamah . And Lamech said to his wives, “ Adah and Zillah , listen to my voice; wives of Lamech , pay attention to my 'imrat . For I killed a man for my wound, and a young man for my bruise. If Cain will be avenged seven times, then Lamech seventy and seven times.” And Adam had sex with his wife again, and she gave birth to a son and called his name Seth , because “ elohim has appointed for me another offspring in place of Abel , because Cain killed him.” And to Seth too, a son was born, and he called his name Enosh . Then people began to call on the name of Yahweh .

A name and its reason

Each naming speech ties the name to a similar-sounding Hebrew verb: קַיִן … קָנִיתִי (verse 1) and שֵׁת … שָׁת־לִי (verse 25). The sound-link exists only in the Hebrew; the English "Cain" and "acquired", "Seth" and "appointed" do not show it.

  • Genesis 4:1: She said, “I have acquired a man with Yahweh.”
  • Genesis 4:25: and called his name Seth, because “ elohim has appointed for me another offspring in place of Abel, because Cain killed him.”
