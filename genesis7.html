Then Yahweh said to Noah , “Come you and all your household, into the ark, because I have seen you to be righteous before me in this generation. Take for yourself from every clean animal, seven pairs, a male and its mate; and from the animals that are not clean, two, a male and its mate. Also, from the birds of the sky, seven pairs, male and female, to keep offspring alive on the surface of the whole earth. For in seven more days, I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe away every living thing that I made from the surface of the ground.” And Noah did everything that Yahweh had commanded him. And Noah was six hundred years old, and the mabul was water over the earth. Then Noah and his sons and his wife and the wives of his sons with him went into the ark because of the waters of the mabul . From the clean animals and from the animals that were not clean, and from the birds and everything that moves on the ground, two by two they came to Noah into the ark, male and female, as elohim had commanded Noah . And it happened after seven days: the waters of the mabul were on the earth. In the six hundredth year of Noah 's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on this day, all the springs of the great deep burst open, and the windows of the sky were opened. And the rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights. On that very day, Noah and Shem and Ham and Japheth , the sons of Noah , and the wife of Noah , and the three wives of his sons with them, went into the ark. They, and all the wild animals according to their kind, and all the livestock according to their kind, and every creeping creature that creeps on the earth according to its kind, and every flying creature according to its kind, every bird of every wing. And they came to Noah into the ark, two by two, from all flesh that has within it ruach life. And those entering, male and female from all flesh, entered just as elohim had commanded him. And Yahweh shut him in. And the mabul lasted forty days on the earth. The waters increased and lifted the vessel, and it rose above the earth. The waters grew stronger and increased greatly on the earth, and the vessel moved on the surface of the waters. And the waters grew stronger very, very much on the earth, and all the high mountains that were under the whole sky were covered. The waters rose fifteen cubits higher, and the mountains were covered. And all flesh moving on the earth, among the birds, the livestock, the wild animals, and all the swarming creatures swarming on the earth, and all humans, died. Everything that had nishmat ruach of life in its nostrils, from all that was on dry ground, died. And he wiped out all living things that were on the surface of the ground— from humans to livestock, to crawling creatures, and to the birds of the sky. They were wiped out from the earth. Only Noah and those with him in the ark remained. And the waters remained powerful over the earth for a hundred and fifty days.
