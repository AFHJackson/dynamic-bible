When humanity began to multiply across the surface of the ground, and daughters were born to them, the sons of the elohim saw that the daughters of humanity were beautiful, and took wives for themselves from all whom they chose. And Yahweh said, “ My ruach will not rule in humanity forever, because it too is flesh. And its days will be one hundred and twenty years.” The giants were on the earth in those days, and also after that, when the sons of the elohim would go to the daughters of humanity, and they bore children for them. These were the mighty men who were from long ago, men of renown. Yahweh saw that humanity’s wickedness was great on the earth, and every inclination of the thoughts of its heart was only evil all day long. And Yahweh regretted that he had made humankind on the earth, and he was deeply pained in his heart. And Yahweh said, “I will wipe away humankind whom I created from the surface of the ground— from humans to livestock, to crawling creatures, and to the birds of the sky— because I regret that I made them.” But Noah found favor in the eyes of Yahweh . These are the generations of Noah . Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generations. Noah walked with the elohim . And Noah fathered three sons: Shem , Ham , and Japheth . And the earth had become ruined before the elohim , and the earth was filled with violence. And elohim saw the earth, and look, it was ruined, because all flesh had ruined its way on the earth. And elohim said to Noah , “The end of all flesh has come before me, because the earth was filled with violence because of them. And look, I am about to destroy them along with the earth.” Make for yourself a vessel of resinous wood. Make the vessel with compartments, and coat it inside and outside with pitch. And this is how you are to make it: the vessel's length will be three hundred cubits, its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits. Make a light opening for the vessel, and finish it to one cubit from the top. Put the opening of the vessel in its side. Make it with lower, second, and third levels. And I, look, am bringing the mabul —water upon the earth— to destroy all flesh that has within it ruach life, from beneath the heavens. Everything that is on the earth will die. And I will establish my covenant with you. And you will enter into the vessel, you and your sons and your wife and the wives of your sons with you. And from everything living, from all flesh, bring two of each into the vessel to keep them alive with you. They are to be male and female. From the birds according to their kind, and from the livestock according to their kind, from every creeping creature of the ground according to their kind, two of each will come to you to be kept alive. And you, take for yourself some of every food that can be eaten, and gather it to yourself. It will be for you and for them to eat. And Noah did everything that elohim commanded him; that is how he did it.
