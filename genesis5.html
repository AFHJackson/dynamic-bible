This is the record of the descendants of Adam . On the day elohim created humanity, he made him in the likeness of elohim . Male and female he created them, and he yvarekh them. And he called their name humanity on the day they were created. Adam lived a hundred and thirty years and fathered a son in his likeness, like his image, and called his name Seth . And the days of Adam after he fathered Seth were eight hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters. All the days of Adam that he lived were nine hundred years and thirty years, and he died. And Seth lived one hundred and five years, and he fathered Enosh . And Seth lived after he fathered Enosh eight hundred and seven years, and he fathered sons and daughters. And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died. And Enosh lived ninety years, and he fathered Kenan . And Enosh lived after he fathered Kenan eight hundred and fifteen years, and he fathered sons and daughters. All the days of Enosh were nine hundred and five years, and he died. Kenan lived seventy years, and he fathered Mahalalel . Kenan lived eight hundred and forty years after he fathered Mahalalel , and he fathered sons and daughters. All the days of Kenan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died. Mahalalel lived sixty-five years, and he fathered Jared . And Mahalalel lived thirty years and eight hundred years after he fathered Jared , and he fathered sons and daughters. And all the days of Mahalalel were ninety-five years and eight hundred years, and he died. And Jared lived sixty-two years and a hundred years, and he fathered Enoch . And Jared lived after he fathered Enoch eight hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters. And all the days of Jared were sixty-two years and nine hundred years, and he died. Enoch lived sixty-five years, and he fathered Methuselah . And Enoch walked with the elohim after he fathered Methuselah for three hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters. And all the days of Enoch were sixty-five years and three hundred years. And Enoch walked with the elohim and he was no longer there, because elohim had taken him. And Methuselah lived eighty-seven years and a hundred years, and he fathered Lamech . And Methuselah lived after he fathered Lamech eighty-two years and seven hundred years, and he fathered sons and daughters. And all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died. And Lamech lived eighty-two years and one hundred years, and he fathered a son. And he called his name Noah , saying, “This one will bring us relief from our work and from the painful toil of our hands, from the ground that Yahweh has cursed.” And Lamech lived after he fathered Noah five hundred and ninety-five years, and he fathered sons and daughters. And all the days of Lamech were seventy-seven years and seven hundred years, and he died. And Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah fathered Shem , Ham , and Japheth .
