And elohim remembered Noah and all the wild animals and all the livestock that were with him in the ark. And elohim made ruach pass over the earth, and the waters subsided. The springs of the deep and the openings of the sky were closed, and the rain from the sky was held back. And the waters receded from over the earth, continually retreating. And the waters had diminished at the end of one hundred and fifty days. And the ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on the mountains of Ararat . And the waters kept diminishing until the tenth month. In the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains became visible. At the end of forty days, Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made. He sent out the raven, and it went out, going out and returning until the water had dried up from over the earth. He sent out the dove from him to see whether the water had receded from over the surface of the ground. But the dove found no resting place for the sole of its foot, so it returned to him, to the ark, because water was over the surface of the whole earth. He reached out his hand and took it and brought it to him, into the ark. He waited another seven days, then again sent out the dove from the ark. The dove came to him at evening, and there was a freshly plucked olive leaf in her beak. Then Noah knew that the water had receded from over the earth. He waited another seven days, then sent out the dove, and she did not return to him ever again. And it happened in the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the water had dried up from over the earth. And Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked, and there it was: the surface of the ground had dried up. And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry. Then elohim spoke to Noah , saying: Go out from the ark, you and your wife and your sons and the wives of your sons with you. Bring out with you every living creature that is with you, from all flesh: birds and livestock and every creeping creature that creeps on the earth. Let them swarm on the earth and reproduce and grow numerous on the earth. Then Noah went out, and his sons and his wife and the wives of his sons with him. Every living creature, every creeping creature, and every bird— everything that moves on the earth— went out from the ark by their families. Then Noah built an altar for Yahweh and took from all the clean livestock and from all the clean birds and offered burnt offerings on the altar. Then Yahweh smelled the soothing scent, and Yahweh said to his heart, “I will not curse the ground again because of humankind, because the inclination of the human heart is evil from youth. And I will not strike down every living thing again as I have done.” For as long as all the days of the earth last, sowing and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will not stop.

The roots יסף and עוֹד form a negative frame: לֹא־יָסְפָה עוֹד (the dove, verse 12), לֹא־אֹסִף … עוֹד (twice in Yahweh's promise, verse 21).

Heart · candidate grouping

לֵב occurs twice in Yahweh's speech: "said to his heart" (אֶל־לִבּוֹ) and "the inclination of the human heart" (לֵב הָאָדָם). Same word, different hearts; candidate grouping, not a claim.

Four pairs of seasons

Four parallel pairs, each two nouns joined by וְ: sowing and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night.

  • Genesis 8:22: sowing and harvest,
  • Genesis 8:22: cold and heat,
  • Genesis 8:22: summer and winter,
  • Genesis 8:22: day and night

Cross-references

Shares words with Genesis 7:4: “For in seven more days, I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe away every living thing that I made from the surface of the ground.”. Shared words: “still”, “day;_when”, “earth”, “night”.
