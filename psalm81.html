Psalm 81

Hebrew numbering, including the superscription in verse 1. Septuagint Psalm 80. English is rendered from the Hebrew. Tap a verse number for the original Hebrew, earlier versions and resource evidence.

Beside elohim: SG singular agreement, PL plural agreement, ? checked, undetermined.

For the music director, on the Gittith. For Asaph . Sing joyfully to elohim , our strength; shout to elohei Jacob ! Raise a song and sound the hand drum, the pleasant-sounding lyre with the harp. Blow the horn at the new moon, at the full moon, for the day of our festival. For it is a decree for Israel , a ruling for elohei Jacob . He established it as a testimony in Joseph when he went out against the land of Egypt . I hear a language I did not know. I freed his shoulder from the burden; his hands moved away from the basket. In distress you called, and I rescued you. I answered you in the hiding place of thunder; I tested you at the waters of Meribah . Selah. Listen, my people, and let me warn you. Israel , if only you would listen to me! There must not be a strange El among you, and you must not bow down to a foreign El . I am Yahweh , your elohim , who brought you up from the land of Egypt . Open wide your mouth, and I will fill it. But my people did not listen to my voice, and Israel was not willing toward me. So I let them go in the stubbornness of their hearts; they would walk according to their own plans. If only my people would listen to me, Israel would walk in my ways! I would subdue their enemies quickly, and turn my hand against their foes. Those who hate Yahweh would pretend to submit to him, and may their time last forever. And he fed him with the richest wheat, and from a rock I will satisfy you with honey.
