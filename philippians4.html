Companion or name?

“True companion” translates gnēsie syzyge, “genuine yokefellow”: one who pulls in the same yoke as Paul. Most translations read it as a description of a fellow worker the Philippians would recognize. Some scholars read Syzygus as his personal name, with “true” as a play on it (a Yokefellow true to his name); the name has not been found elsewhere. Scholars are split.
