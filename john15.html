I am the true vine, and my Father is the vine grower. Every branch in me that does not produce fruit, he removes it, and every branch that produces fruit, he prunes it clean so that it may produce more fruit. You are already clean because of the logon that I have spoken to you. Remain in me, and I in you. Just as the branch cannot produce fruit on its own unless it remains in the vine, so neither can you unless you remain in me. I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever remains in me, and I in him, that person produces much fruit, because apart from me you can do nothing. If anyone does not remain in me, he is thrown outside like a branch and dries up. And they gather them and throw them into the fire, and they burn. If you remain in me and my rhēmata remain in you, ask for whatever you want, and it will happen for you. My Father is glorified in this: that you produce much fruit and become my disciples. Just as the Father ēgapēsen me, I too ēgapēsa you. Remain in my agapē . If you keep my commandments, you will remain in my agapē , just as I have kept my Father’s commandments and remain in his agapē . I have spoken these things to you so that my joy may be in you and your joy may be complete.

This is my commandment: that you agapate one another, just as I ēgapēsa you. No one has greater agapēn than this: that someone lay down his psychēn for his philōn . You are my philoi if you do what I command you. I no longer call you doulous , because the doulos does not oiden what his master is doing. But I have called you philous , because everything that I heard from my Father I made known to you. You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you so that you would go and produce fruit, and your fruit would remain, so that whatever you ask the Father in my name, he would give you. I command you these things, so that you agapate one another.

If the kosmos hates you, ginōskete that it has hated me before you. If you were from the kosmou , the kosmos would ephilei its own. But because you are not from the kosmou , but I chose you out of the kosmou , for this reason the kosmos hates you. Remember the logou that I said to you: ‘A doulos is not greater than his master.’ If they persecuted me, they will persecute you too. If they kept my logon , they will keep yours too. But they will do all these things to you because of my name, because they do not oidasin the one who sent me. If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have hamartian . But now they have no excuse for their hamartias . Whoever hates me also hates my Father. If I had not done among them the works that no one allos did, they would not have hamartian . But now they both heōrakasin and have hated both me and my Father. But so that the logos written in their law would be fulfilled: ‘They hated me for no reason.’

When the Advocate comes, whom I will send to you from the Father, the pneuma of truth that proceeds from the Father, he will testify about me. And you too testify, because you have been with me from the beginning.
