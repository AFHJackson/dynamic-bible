Psalm 82

Hebrew numbering, eight verses. Septuagint Psalm 81. English is rendered from the Hebrew. Tap a verse number for the original Hebrew, earlier versions and resource evidence.

Beside elohim: SG singular agreement, PL plural agreement, ? checked, undetermined.

A psalm for Asaph . elohim stands in the assembly of El ; he judges among elohim . How long will you judge unjustly and show favor to the wicked? Selah. Give justice to the weak and the fatherless; uphold the rights of the afflicted and the poor. Rescue the weak and the needy; save them from the hand of the wicked. They do not know and do not understand; they walk about in darkness. All the foundations of the earth are shaking. I said, “ You are elohim and sons of Elyon , all of you.” Yet you will die like humans, and you will fall like one of the rulers. Rise, elohim , judge the earth, for you will inherit among all the nations.
