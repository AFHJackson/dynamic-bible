Finally, my brothers and sisters, rejoice in the Lord. Writing the same things to you is no trouble for me, but for you it is a safeguard.

blepete the dogs; blepete the evil workers; blepete the cutting. For we are the circumcision, those who worship by God’s pneumati and boast in Christ Jesus and are not confident in flesh, although I have confidence in flesh too. If anyone allos thinks they can be confident in flesh, I can even more: circumcised at eight days old, from the people of Israel, the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew from Hebrews; as for the law, a Pharisee; as for zeal, persecuting the assembly; as for righteousness under the law, having become blameless.

But whatever was gain to me, these things I have counted as loss because of Christ. Indeed, I also count everything as loss because of the surpassing value of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord. Because of him I suffered the loss of everything, and I count it as garbage, so that I may gain Christ and be found in him, having no righteousness of my own from the law, but the righteousness through the pisteōs of Christ, the righteousness from God on the pistei —to gnōnai him and the dynamin of his resurrection and sharing in his sufferings, being conformed to his death, if somehow I may reach the resurrection from the dead.

Not that I have already received it or already been made perfect, but I press on to see if I can also take hold of it, because I too was taken hold of by Christ. Brothers and sisters, I do not consider myself to have taken hold of it. But one thing: forgetting what is behind and stretching forward to what is ahead, I press on toward the goal for the prize of God’s upward call in Christ Jesus. So, as many of us as are perfect, let us think this. And if you think differently about anything, God will reveal this too to you. Still, let us keep in step with the same point we have reached.

Become fellow imitators of me, brothers and sisters, and watch those who live this way, just as you have us as an example. For many live as enemies of the cross of Christ. I often told you about them, and now I say it even weeping. Their end is destruction, their god is their belly, and their glory is in their shame. They set their thinking on earthly things. For our citizenship is in the heavens, from where we also eagerly await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, who will reshape the body of our humiliation, conformed to the body of his glory, through the working by which he is able even to bring all things under himself.
