To the end; It is finished

“To the end” (13:1, eis telos) and “It is finished” (19:30, tetelestai) are two forms of the same Greek root, tel-, “end, completion”: telos is the noun and teleō, “bring to its end, finish”, is the verb. Telos can also mean “to the uttermost, completely”. John opens the Book of Glory saying that Jesus ēgapēsen his own “to the end” (13:1), and Jesus’ last word on the cross is “It is finished” (19:30; 19:28 has the root twice, “everything was already finished … so that the Scripture would be finished”). The two verses mark the beginning and the end of the same love.
