From above, or again

“From above” translates anōthen, a word that also means “again” or “anew”. Jesus means from above: “the one who comes from above” (3:31), and authority given “from above” (19:11). Nicodemus hears “again”, so in 3:4 he asks how anyone can enter his mother’s womb “a second time”. This translation says “from above” here and in 3:7, so his misunderstanding shows as it does in the Greek.

Lifted up

“Lifted up” translates one Greek verb, hypsoō, which means both raised up high and exalted.
